🔵 ABOUT DELHI CAPITALS

Delhi Capitals, formerly known as Delhi Daredevils, have been part of the IPL since 2008. Though they are yet to win an IPL title, DC has produced some of the most exciting young talents and memorable performances in IPL history. Their best season came in 2020 when they reached their first IPL final.
